# Wuu brand assets

- `wuu.svg` is exported from the desktop mascot's existing identity: `WUU_MASCOT_NAME`, `WUU_MASCOT_DEFAULT_HUE`, `WUU_MASCOT_TRAITS`, and `WUU_MASCOT_IDENTITY_PERSPECTIVE`. The geometry comes from blobatar; keep `LICENSE-blobatar.txt` with redistributions.
- `wuu.png` is a transparent 512 px rendering of that SVG.
- `dusk.webp`, `shore.webp`, and `meadow.webp` are crops from the user's approved ImageGen triptych, based on their own coastal photographs. The original source photographs remain with the creator. These are artistic reinterpretations, not documentary photographs. Do not apply the blobatar license to the photography.
- Use dusk for spacious hero compositions, shore as the main photographic identity, and meadow for quieter supporting sections. Keep important content in HTML, with contrast checked against its actual placement.

The generation brief was: preserve the recognizable landscapes of three supplied photographs; reinterpret the grassy mountain as a soft analog living-systems image, the yellow stairs and turquoise volcanic coast as sea-glass teal with warm ochre, and the rocky open sea as a quiet blue-hour image. Fine grain, restrained light, simplified foregrounds, no logos or text.
